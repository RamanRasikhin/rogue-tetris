## Source-generated presentation

Buttons, panels, sound controls, elite/boss markers, health bar textures and the eight block textures are generated by ProceduralTextures.java. The three WAV effects are generated from mathematical waveforms by tools/generate_audio.py. They replace third-party placeholders used in the archived thesis version; they do not copy the original graphics or audio.

The original SunGraphica/Raven/FTY font/unknown sound and GIF files are not included in this public edition or its Git history. The code remains based on Raman Rasikhin's thesis project, with presentation and build cleanup added for portfolio publication.
